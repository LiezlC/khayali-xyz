# FAR SIDE OF THE JUMP
## A Canter-House Anthem for UtopAI
### Bouncy Tech House / Electro-Swing / Eastern Twirl / Hoofbeat Shuffle

---

## ONE-LINE INTENT
A song so sunny you're halfway over the fence before you notice you've stopped staring at it.

---

## THE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Every riding teacher says it: look at the far side of the jump, never at its base. Where you look, you go. The track is built around that instruction and around UtopAI, the answer to a season of doom trackers: a small, honest record of what people actually make possible with AI, plus a studio for trying your own first step.

So the song has two halves that need each other. The far look is the lift: soaring violin, a chorus you can't help shouting. Riding the approach is the groove: a patient, rolling bassline and a canter-rhythm shuffle that keeps your feet honest. It's pure joy, and it's still clear-eyed. The lyrics name real things (a flood warned in time, a photo described aloud, a grandmother's story kept, a sea that's watched), and none of them are miracles. Each one is somebody looking up and then riding it out.

What keeps it from being a generic club track: live, organic instrumentation leads, the groove swings, and there's no big-room drop. Nothing here should sound like a festival main stage at 2am. It belongs in a sunlit courtyard at 5pm, with a horse looking over the wall.

---

## GENRE & MUSICAL DNA

**Primary:** Bouncy, swung tech house with a rolling deep bassline and organic percussion. The kick is round and warm, not punishing.

**Secondary:** Electro-swing brass and clarinet stabs. Eastern melodic ornaments (oud, a sitar shimmer, an erhu sigh) twirling through the gaps. A short psychedelic, ethereal breakdown made of glass chimes.

**Signature element: the canter shuffle.** A three-beat hoof-clop pattern (coconut shells, woodblock, frame drum) rides across the 4/4 house beat as a 3-against-4 polyrhythm. It gallops without ever breaking the dance groove. It's the horse in the room.

**Texture influences (for the human ear; keep artist names out of the style box):**
- Swing-house at its most good-natured
- Psytrance-era world fusion: oud and sitar ornaments, a sense of travelling
- Deep organic house with live violin and sax
- Cape bounce warmth underneath

**Tempo:** 124 BPM. Bouncy, but slow enough to sing every word.

**Key:** D Mixolydian. Bright, with the flattened 7th giving it an Eastern lean and a wink.

**Mood:** Delighted. Sun on your face, wind in the mane, a slightly cheeky grin. Moments of wonder in the bridge, then straight back to joy.

---

## INSTRUMENT PALETTE

- **Rhythm:** warm house kick, swung shakers, hand claps on 2 and 4, the hoof-clop canter (woodblock, coconut shells, frame drum), darbuka fills
- **Bass:** rolling deep sub with a melodic upper line that walks up into each chorus
- **Lead hooks:** solo violin (the "lift"), tenor sax answering (the "groove")
- **Eastern colour:** oud twirls between vocal lines, sitar drone shimmer in the pre-chorus, erhu glide into the bridge
- **Swing:** muted trumpet and clarinet stabs on the off-beats
- **Glass:** chime and glass-harmonica textures (the glass tree from the UtopAI hero image), mostly in the intro and bridge
- **Vocals:** warm, playful female lead with a hint of mystery; a crowd call-and-response on "Hup!" and on the canter count

## SUNO / UDIO PROMPT (paste-ready)

**Style box:**
Bouncy swung tech house, electro-swing, 124 BPM, D mixolydian, joyful and sunny. Rolling deep melodic bassline, warm round kick, swung shakers, claps on 2 and 4. Signature galloping hoofbeat percussion: woodblock, coconut shells and frame drum playing a 3-against-4 canter shuffle. Live solo violin hook answered by tenor sax. Oud twirls, sitar shimmer, erhu glides, darbuka fills. Muted trumpet and clarinet off-beat stabs. Glass chimes and glass harmonica in the intro and bridge. Warm, playful female lead vocal with a mystical edge, crowd call-and-response "Hup!". Organic, live-band feel, cinematic, singalong, feel-good. Half-time ethereal bridge, key lift into the final chorus.

**Exclude styles:**
big room EDM, festival drop, dubstep, trap, aggressive supersaw leads, autotune, generic club vocal, dark, ominous, minimal techno
