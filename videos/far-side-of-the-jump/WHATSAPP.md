New khayali tune, and a fun one to build 🐴✨

*Eyes on the Far Side*: a riding-lesson song for the age of AI doom trackers. Look at the far side of the jump and you clear it.

🎥 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b-pmjEYgoEo

*How it was made*
• Music: Suno, from a prompt and lyrics written with Claude
• Paintings: Codex (gpt-image-2), each scene repainted as separate transparent layers so code could move them in parallax, plus a 4-frame gallop cycle for the pony
• Animation: Claude in Claude Code, built with HyperFrames (HTML compositions rendered to video), no video model involved
• Timing: Parakeet transcription for the lyrics, a librosa beat map for the cuts, so the coded glass tree blooms on the actual beats

8,267 frames, about 50 minutes to render on my laptop.
