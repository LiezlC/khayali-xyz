## Description

Every riding teacher says it: look at the far side of the jump, never at the pole. Where you look, you go.

A ten-year-old on a borrowed spotted mare learns it in the ring. The rest of us are learning it in a season of doom trackers. The best of them, like Failsafe, log every AI failure with its source and how long it ran before anyone noticed. UtopAI turns that same care toward the far side of the fence: a small, sourced record of what people already make possible with AI, plus a studio for sketching your own first step.
