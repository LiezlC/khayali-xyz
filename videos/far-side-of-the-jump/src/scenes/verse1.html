Ten years old on a borrowed mare,
fence comin’ up and I froze right there,
teacher hollered from across the ring:
“Don’t watch the pole, it’s a hungry thing!”
So I lifted my chin to the open sky,
picked out a tree on the other side,
heels down, hands soft, heart in my throat,
and over we sailed like a paper boat!
