## Post

Every riding teacher I had said the same thing: look at the far side of the jump. Stare at the pole and you hit it.

AI governance has built superb pole-watchers. Failsafe and the AI Incident Database log failures with their sources and the people who caught them. That rigor deserves copying, and it rarely gets pointed at the benefits.

Most AI good news arrives as a press release. Two decades of checking benefits on the ground around extractive projects taught me the question that matters: did it reach the people it was meant for, and who says so?

UtopAI is a small, open attempt at that standard for AI benefits. Ten sourced records so far, each with its evidence and its limits, from flood forecasts for ungauged rivers to African-language research led by its speakers. A studio sits alongside, for turning “what would you love to make possible?” into one small experiment you can try this week.

Some arguments travel further with a horse in them, so there’s also a song. Eyes on the Far Side follows a ten-year-old on a borrowed spotted mare toward a glass tree full of real projects.
